Four ways to fold the goals into the header

All four drop the big headline entirely. The page opens with one plain sentence saying what SRI-2030 is, then the goals sit immediately beneath it carrying small marks. What differs is how those goals are set.

A is the one currently built as the Compact version. Say which you prefer and I'll swap it in — they are interchangeable, and none of them affects the rest of the page.

A

Marked listbuilt

Plainest. Four rows, each with a small square in one of the logo colours. Reads as a quiet list of commitments.

SRI-2030An initiative of The Downforce Trust

SRI-2030 works with governments, mainly in Africa, to scale the System of Rice Intensification — a way of growing more rice from less seed, less water and less energy, with lower emissions.

  • 50 million hectares of SRI by 2030
  • Africa self-sufficient in rice
  • Adoption at national scale, with government
  • Better returns for smallholders

Notes

On the marks. A, C and D use plain coloured squares, which match the squares already used on the section numbers further down the page. B uses the logo's four tile glyphs instead — prettier, but worth knowing that those same glyphs also label the four SRI principles in the methodology section, so using them here too may imply a mapping between goals and principles that doesn't exist.

On the wording. The statement and the four goals are newly written rather than lifted from the current site. Every claim traces back to SRI-2030's own material, but the phrasing is ours and needs their sign-off.
